# Selection & clipboard

Drag or Shift+arrows to select a cell range. `Ctrl+C` / `Ctrl+X` / `Ctrl+V` are the standard shortcuts (`Alt+*` remain deprecated aliases).

<LiveDemo name="selection" />

Clipboard options: `copyFormattedValue`, `copyHeadersToClipboard`, `copyDigitsAsLatin`, `pasteAddsRows`, `onClipboardCopy` / `onClipboardPaste`.
